**Date completed:** November 4, 2026  
**Path / Module:** Introduction to Cyber Security

## Objective
Explore the wide range of career paths available in cybersecurity and understand that the field is much broader than just “hacking.”

## Key Things I Learned
- There is a large shortage of cybersecurity professionals and many open roles
- Cybersecurity offers strong pay even at entry level compared to many other fields
- Major role categories include:
  - Security Analyst (monitoring and investigating alerts)
  - Security Engineer (building and maintaining defenses)
  - Incident Responder (handling active attacks)
  - Digital Forensics Examiner
  - Malware Analyst
  - Penetration Tester
  - Red Teamer
- The industry needs people from many different backgrounds, not only traditional computer science paths
